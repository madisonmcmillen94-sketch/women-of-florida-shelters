About Us

What This Site Is

Women of Florida Shelters is a peer navigation hub built to help survivors of domestic violence find the FCADV-certified shelter that serves their county, quickly and without barriers. We don't operate a shelter ourselves — we maintain this directory, map, and resource guide so that finding help doesn't add another obstacle to an already hard moment.

This site is a project of WOFS Women of Florida Shelters, a Florida-based nonprofit. Our 501(c)(3) status and EIN are currently in progress; we'll update this page once they're finalized.

Our Mission

Why We Do This

To make it faster and safer for survivors across Florida to find the help that's already out there — by keeping an accurate, easy-to-search directory of certified shelters and putting practical safety, legal, and financial resources in one place, at no cost to the people who need them.

Contact Us

Reach Us

Have a correction, a shelter to add, or a question about this site? Reach us at contact@womenoffloridashelters.org.

If you are in immediate danger, please call 911. For 24/7 confidential support, call Florida's statewide domestic violence hotline at 1-800-500-1119.

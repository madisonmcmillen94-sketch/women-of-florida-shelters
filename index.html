Did you know?

You may be eligible for Crime Victim Compensation

Covers medical bills, counseling, lost wages, and relocation costs for survivors of domestic violence. Call 1-800-226-6667.

Learn more →

A protective order is free — no lawyer required

Florida's Injunction for Protection Against Domestic Violence can be filed at your county courthouse at no cost.

Learn more →

Texting can be safer than calling

Text START to 88788 to reach the National Domestic Violence Hotline if your phone might be monitored.

Learn more →

Your kids can enroll in school immediately

Federal law lets children displaced by domestic violence enroll in a new school right away — no records required up front.

Have a safety plan ready

A go-bag, a safe contact, and a plan for digital safety can make leaving faster if you ever need to.

About Women Of Florida Shelters

Women Of Florida Shelters (WOFS) is an independent community awareness initiative and peer resource hub designed to help unhoused individuals, survivors, and families navigate state housing systems, county assistance programs, and emergency shelter care.

WOFS is not a direct financial solicitor or shelter operator. To ensure 100% transparency and compliance with Florida laws, our platform directs all public financial support directly to certified, registered 501(c)(3) emergency shelters and community partners serving Florida counties.

Florida SHIP Housing Assistance Checklist

Required documentation when applying for county rental move-in or down payment assistance

Category 1

Identification

  • ✅ Unexpired FL Driver's License or Photo ID (all adults 18+)
  • ✅ Social Security Cards for every household member
  • ✅ Birth Certificates for all minor children
Category 2

Income Verification

  • ✅ Past 3 months of pay stubs (if employed)
  • ✅ SSA / SSI / SSDI Award Letter (current year)
  • ✅ 3-6 months of complete bank statements
  • ✅ SNAP/Medicaid DCF Benefit Statements
Category 3

Housing & Priority

  • ✅ Signed Shelter Residency Verification Letter
  • ✅ Proposed Lease or Landlord Intent Form
  • ✅ Disability Medical Verification (Form CF-ES 2314)
